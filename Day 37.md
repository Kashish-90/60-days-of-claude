📚 What I Learned
1️⃣ Task Ownership
Every task should have a clear owner. Without ownership, responsibilities become unclear and work gets delayed.
2️⃣ Task Routing
Not every task belongs to the same team. Learning how to route tasks to the right department improves efficiency.
3️⃣ Team Collaboration
Complex problems often require multiple teams working together. Communication and coordination are critical skills.
4️⃣ Systems Thinking
Instead of focusing on a single task, I learned to view how different parts of an organization connect and contribute to overall success.
5️⃣ Learning Through Simulation
Interactive simulations make workplace concepts easier to understand than reading theory alone.
💭 Reflection
Today's challenge showed me that technical skills are important, but understanding how teams work together and how decisions flow through an organization is equally valuable. These concepts will help me in future internships, projects, and professional environments.
